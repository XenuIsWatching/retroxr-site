v0.3.0 is out, on [SideQuest](https://sidequestvr.com/app/61366) and on
[GitHub](https://github.com/XenuIsWatching/RetroXR/releases/latest). It is the biggest
release so far, and most of it is about making the hardware behave more like hardware.

## RetroAchievements

Sign in with your [RetroAchievements](https://retroachievements.org) account and
achievements sync as you play, with notifications and rich presence. You can also browse a
game's achievements from the menu without launching it.

Hardcore mode is not supported yet — RetroAchievements requires a project to have existed
for six months before enabling it, so that one is a matter of waiting.

## PS1 memory cards

PlayStation memory cards are now real objects. You slot one into the console by hand, and
there is a manager for them: keep several saves on one card, move a save to another card,
back it up to RomM, or delete it.

This is the pattern the whole project is chasing — the save is a thing you can hold and
lose, not a hidden file.

## A Wii you have to set up properly

There is now a Wii, with a sensor bar you place on top of your TV.

Spawn a Wii and a Wiimote, then press the **sync** button on both to pair them — the Wii
first, exactly as you would have. Pick the Wiimote up and swing it. A Wii MotionPlus can
be spawned and attached to the Wiimote for the games that wanted one.

The Wii needs its composite adapter for A/V out, which is spawnable too.

[There's a video of it in action.](https://youtu.be/PHeaNuKQqNM)

Nunchuk support is not in, because libretro has no standard way to expose a second
accelerometer on a controller port. A proposal upstream is the way that gets fixed.

Dolphin is now downloaded as a custom fork carrying an Android race-condition fix, IR
passthrough for the sensor bar, and MotionPlus gyro fixes.

## A 3DS in actual 3D

The 3DS core is now a custom build that produces the stereoscopic effect — which, in a
headset, is the entire reason to emulate a 3DS. Download it from the core manager.

The patch has been merged upstream; nightly builds carrying it just were not available
when this shipped.

## The television grew up

- **Four composite inputs.** No more unplugging one console to watch another — leave them
  all wired and cycle inputs with the source button on the set or the remote.
- **Aspect ratio button**, switching between 16:9 and 4:3.
- **An NES RF adapter** for the antenna input, for machines that never had phono out.
- **HDHomeRun support** — if you have one on your network with an antenna attached, RetroXR
  finds it, and the channel up and down buttons tune through your actual local channels.

## Desktop computers

A monitor, speakers with a real volume knob, and the connectors to go with them: PS/2
mouse and keyboard plugs, a VGA DE-15 port, and a 3.5 mm audio jack.

## Bring your own controller

Spawn a **Controller Receiver** from the Controllers tab, plug it into a system's
controller port, and your own Bluetooth or USB gamepad drives that port — detected
automatically, and with nothing held in your hands. Two receivers means two players.

## Menu and quality-of-life

- Platforms with no core downloaded now show up in purple, so you can see at a glance what
  is playable.
- Compact tiles, and the ability to hide systems you do not care about.
- The options menu is split into tabs.
- A cartridge's saves are reachable straight from the spawn menu.
- Sixteen more systems are listed in their own right, including Satellaview, Famicom Disk
  System, 64DD, SuperGrafx, PCE-CD, 32X, SG-1000, Game Gear and Sega CD.
- A haptic tick when a VR hand presses a key, a button or a mouse click.

## Fixes

Numerous libretro crashes, better Vulkan synchronization, multi-disk games retrieving the
right disc image rather than disc one every time, multi-file RomM downloads, and several
room save/load fixes.

## Known issues

- **Dolphin with passthrough and Vulkan** leaves artifacts on Quest. Select **OpenGL ES 3**
  as the preferred hardware renderer for Dolphin in the core manager.
- **Netplay is extremely experimental.** It is in the build; it is not a feature yet.

The [full changelog](https://github.com/XenuIsWatching/RetroXR/compare/v0.2.0...v0.3.0) has
everything.
