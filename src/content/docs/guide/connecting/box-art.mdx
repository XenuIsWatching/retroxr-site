A shelf of identical gray cartridges is not much of a shelf. RetroXR can fetch cover art,
screenshots, game details and **scanned manuals** from
[ScreenScraper](https://screenscraper.fr), so your library looks like a library.

## Setting up your account

ScreenScraper accounts are free, and worth the two minutes — anonymous scraping is heavily
rate-limited.

<Steps>

1. Register at [screenscraper.fr](https://screenscraper.fr), then open the menu in RetroXR
   and go to `OPTIONS`.

2. Pick the **Scraper** tab, marked ① below.

3. Enter your **Username (ssid)** and **Password** in the fields marked ②.

</Steps>

<Image src={credentials} alt="The OPTIONS screen with the Scraper tab highlighted, and a box around the Username (ssid) and Password fields. Region Priority and Language Priority sit below them." />

Below the credentials are two more settings worth knowing about:

- **Region Priority** — which region's cover you get when several exist. The default is
  `us, eu, jp, ss`.
- **Language Priority** — the same idea for language, defaulting to `en, fr`.

Reorder these if you would rather have European boxes than North American ones.

## Scraping a game

Open `SPAWN` → **Games**, pick a system, and find the game in the list. Each row
carries its own controls on the right.

<Image src={scrapeRom} alt="A row in the Games list for 3-D WorldRunner, with circles around two icons on the right: a database-and-magnifier icon for scraping, and an open-book icon for reading the manual." />

**① — the database-and-magnifier icon starts a scrape** for that one game. RetroXR hashes
the ROM, sends it to ScreenScraper, and fills in whatever comes back.

**② — the open-book icon appears once a manual has been found**, and spawns it as a book
you can hold.

The other two icons on the row are the gamepad, which spawns the cartridge and starts it,
and the list, which reaches that game's saves.

## What you get

- **Box art** on the cartridge itself and in the Games list.
- **Screenshots and game details.**
- **Marquees**, for the arcade systems that had them.
- **Manuals**, which become real books.

## Reading the manual while you play

This is the part worth doing. A scraped manual spawns as a physical book — leave it open
on the desk next to the TV while the game runs, which is what manuals were for.

Page turning, sizing and the half-page option are covered on
[books and magazines](/guide/room/books/).

<Aside type="note">
	ScreenScraper matches on file hashes and names, so an unusually named or heavily patched
	ROM may not be found. That is a limitation of the database rather than of RetroXR.
</Aside>
