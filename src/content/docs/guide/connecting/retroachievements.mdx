[RetroAchievements](https://retroachievements.org) adds achievements to retro games — a
community-built set for thousands of titles, checked as you play. RetroXR signs in to your
account and unlocks them in the headset.

New in [v0.3.0](/blog/).

## Signing in

Accounts are free at [retroachievements.org](https://retroachievements.org).

<Steps>

1. Open the menu and go to `OPTIONS`.
2. Pick the **RetroAchievements** tab.
3. Enter your **username** and **password**.

</Steps>

:::caution[Two different keys, and the wrong one fails silently]
Your RetroAchievements profile also shows a **Web API key**. That is **not** the one that
unlocks achievements — it is a longer value for reading data.

Signing in with it appears to work and then never unlocks anything, which is a
frustrating way to spend an evening. Use your account password.
:::

## What you get

- **Unlocks as you play**, with a toast and the achievement's badge.
- **Rich presence** — your profile shows what you are playing.
- **Browse a game's achievements without launching it**, from the game's own page in the
  menu.

## Seeing a game's achievements

Open `SPAWN` → **Cartridges**, find the game, and press the **list** icon on its row. The
page has two tabs:

<Image src={savesPanel} alt="The page for a game, with two tabs circled: Saves marked 1 and Achievements marked 2." />

**②** is the achievements list. You can read what a game asks of you before deciding to
start it — which is the point of having it here rather than only in a toast mid-game.

**①** is that game's [saves](/guide/connecting/saves/).

## Hardcore mode

**Not supported yet.** RetroAchievements requires a project to have existed for six months
before it can enable hardcore, so this is a matter of waiting rather than of work.

## What works and what does not

Achievement support depends on the **core** and the **game**, exactly as it does in any
other RetroAchievements-capable frontend. A system whose core does not support it will
never report anything, and that is not a RetroXR fault.

If a game you expect to have achievements is not unlocking any, check that the core you
have set as default for that system supports them — see
[downloading and managing cores](/guide/cores/downloading/).

:::caution[Needs an in-headset pass]
The two-key distinction and the hardcore restriction come straight from the source and the
v0.3.0 release notes. The exact placement of the sign-in fields and the appearance of the
unlock toast have not been verified against a running build.
:::
