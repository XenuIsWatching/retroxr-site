The Atari 2600 in RetroXR is the **six-switch VCS** — the "heavy sixer", wood veneer and
all. Along with the NES it is one of the few machines wearing a detailed shell rather than
a procedural stand-in.

## The switches

This is the detail the 2600 is worth spawning for. They are **slide switches**, not
buttons — thin levers that travel up and down the sloped control panel — and the two that
are wired behave differently from each other, as they did on the real thing.

| Switch | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **POWER** | **Latches.** Two detents; it stays where you put it |
| **GAME RESET** | **Momentary.** Push it and it springs back the instant you let go |

<Aside type="note" title="Modeled but not yet wired">
	The other four switches — **TV Type**, **Left Difficulty**, **Right Difficulty** and
	**Game Select** — are modeled and individually pivoted in the shell, but they do not do
	anything yet.
</Aside>

Driving these with the generic button used elsewhere sank them into the shell, which is
both the wrong motion and buries the ON/OFF legend printed beside each slot. They get
their own slider behavior instead.

## Cartridges

	src="/video/atari/atari_cart.mp4"

<Steps>

1. Spawn a game from `SPAWN` → **Games**.
2. Push it into the slot on top of the console.
3. Slide **POWER** on.

</Steps>

There is **no eject** on this machine — 2600 carts pull straight out of the top slot, so
that is how you take them out here too.

## The CX40 joystick

<Turntable
	src="/video/atari/atari_cx40.mp4"
	poster={stickPoster}
	alt="The Atari CX40 joystick rotating: a square black base with a single stick and one orange button in the corner."

/>

Spawn one from `SPAWN` → **Controllers** and plug it into a controller port. Two ports,
so two sticks.

For a real gamepad instead, spawn a **Controller Receiver** and plug that into the port —
see [using a real gamepad, keyboard or mouse](/guide/playing/external-devices/).

## Getting a picture

The 2600 predates composite sockets, so like the NES it reaches a television through an
**RF switch** — see [plugging things in](/guide/playing/plugging-in/) and the
[NES page](/guide/platforms/nes/), which covers the same RF chain in detail.

## Cores

The 2600 has more than one libretro core available. Pick the default in `CORES` →
**Manager** — see [downloading and managing cores](/guide/cores/downloading/).

:::caution[Needs an in-headset pass]
The switch behavior above is read from the model's source and is accurate. Which cores are
offered, and how the RF chain behaves for this specific machine, have not been checked
against a running build.
:::
