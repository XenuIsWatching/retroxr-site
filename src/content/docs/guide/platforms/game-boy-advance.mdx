---
title: Game Boy Advance
description: The Game Boy Advance in RetroXR, and the Card e-Reader that goes into it.
---

The Game Boy Advance plays cartridges from your `roms/game_boy_advance` folder.

What it grew, and what is new in **v0.6.0**, is the
**[Card e-Reader](/guide/platforms/e-reader/)** — a cartridge with a slit through its roof
that you slide printed dotcode cards through, edge first. It has a page of its own, because
which cards it will read depends on how they are named.
