The Genesis — the Mega Drive outside North America — takes cartridges from your
`roms/mega_drive` folder. It is also the console Sega bolted the most hardware onto, and
both of its add-ons are real objects here.

They stack in **opposite directions**, which is what makes the full tower possible:

| Unit | Where it goes | Its media |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **32X** | **Into** the cartridge slot, with its own slot on top | `roms/sega_32x` |
| **Sega CD** | **Underneath** — the console stands on it | `roms/sega_cd` |

See [expansion hardware](/guide/playing/expansion-hardware/) for how a stack goes together.

<Aside type="note" title="The console wears a stand-in">
	The Genesis has no modelled shell yet, so it spawns as the procedural box. The add-ons,
	their slots and the CD tray are all real.
</Aside>

## The 32X

The 32X goes into the cartridge slot like a game, and has **a cartridge slot of its own on
top** — it is the only Sega expansion here that a game cartridge goes *into* rather than
past. Put a 32X cartridge in that upper slot.

A Genesis with a 32X on it is a different machine, and RetroXR switches to **picodrive**,
which is both halves at once. Take the 32X off and the console goes back to its usual core.

## The Sega CD

The Sega CD is a base the console **stands on**, and it loads from a **tray** — press the
unit's own eject button, put a disc in, close it.

On a Sega CD title the disc is the game and the console's cartridge slot stays empty. The
core is **genesis_plus_gx**.

<Aside type="caution" title="It needs a region BIOS">
	The Sega CD will not boot without the BIOS for its region, which you supply:

	| Region | File |
	| --- | --- |
	| Europe | `bios_CD_E.bin` |
	| USA | `bios_CD_U.bin` |
	| Japan | `bios_CD_J.bin` |

	They go in genesis_plus_gx's system folder — the `BIOS / Extras` view in `CORES` shows
	which you have. Note the core declares all of them **optional**, which they are for a
	plain cartridge and are not for a disc.
</Aside>

## The Tower of Power

Both at once: the Sega CD on the bottom, the Genesis standing on it, the 32X in the
console's slot. Sega never sold it as one product and it was famously precarious in real
life — here it simply stacks.

RetroXR treats the full tower as its own machine rather than as two add-ons that happen to
be present. It runs on **picodrive**, and **the disc is still what boots** — the tower
exists so that a Sega CD game can use the 32X's extra hardware, so the CD remains the thing
being played.

<Aside type="caution" title="Not yet verified against real games">
	Every Genesis combination here — 32X alone, Sega CD alone, and the full tower — is
	written from each core's documented behaviour and is marked **unverified** in RetroXR's
	own expansion catalog. The stacking, the media routing and the core switch are
	implemented; what has not been done is sitting down with real discs and cartridges and
	confirming each one boots and plays.

	If you try one, working or broken, that is genuinely useful to hear about on
	[Discord](https://discord.gg/mdjdBDTdW) or in an
	[issue](https://github.com/XenuIsWatching/RetroXR/issues).
</Aside>
